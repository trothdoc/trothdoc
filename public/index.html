Change, with context

A document should have more than a folder full of old copies.

01

Version history

Keep every meaningful revision with its author, timestamp, message, and complete document history.

02

Review changes

Compare revisions, discuss changes, request updates, and approve the version that should move forward.

03

Search the past

Find the change you need by document, author, message, review, comment, or eventually the change itself.

Built around history

Know what changed, who changed it, and why.

04

Every revision has a reason

Attach a clear message to each revision instead of relying on filenames like final-v7-really-final.pdf.

05

Reviews stay with the work

Comments and approvals remain connected to the revision they belong to, not buried in chat or email.

06

A durable audit trail

Follow a document from its earliest revision to the version in front of you today.
